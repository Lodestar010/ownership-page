Yours outright and freely

Own your name. Outright.

Ownership is a naming service built on Arc. A name like yourname.arc is yours — registered on-chain, held in your own wallet, renewable for free, and not rented from a platform that can raise the price or pull the plug.

You hold your own keys. This site never takes custody of anything: it reads the public contracts to check names, and when you register, your wallet signs the transaction. No accounts, no passwords, no middleman.

Look up a name

Check whether a name is free on the Arc testnet. No wallet needed — this reads the public Registry and Registrar contracts directly.

Register this name (testnet)

This registers the name to your connected wallet address for a 2-year term; renewals are free. Payment is testnet USDC sent with the transaction — your wallet will ask you to confirm. Nothing is signed until you approve it there.

Testnet names do not carry over to mainnet. If you want this name on mainnet, you will need to register it again when mainnet launches.
